Rule — Make it matter, Make it fun!

I hold a Bachelor's in Architectural Engineering and am completing a Master's in Design for Built Environment at Federico II University in Naples — while learning to develop iOS apps at the Apple Developer Academy.

My practice lives at the intersection of spatial thinking, technology, and human experience. I approach every product with systems intuition and a deep curiosity for how people move through screens and spaces alike.

The architecture training is the quiet force behind everything. Designing a building teaches you to think in sequences — how someone enters, what they notice first, what they discover last. That same instinct shapes every screen I design. The Apple Developer Academy gave me the technical language to build those experiences myself, not just sketch them. And the art practice keeps the work honest — a reminder that the most effective design is always felt before it's understood.

GraBeat
GraBeat
UI/UX Designer · App Icon Designer
Pinch. Play. Repeat.
🏆 #2 App Store · Top Free Apps

A multiplayer rhythm game built on hand-tracking and music. Players use pinch gestures to catch flying notes in sync with the beat — competing to outscore each other or collaborating to keep a shared Groove Meter alive. Special note mechanics introduce strategic twists that can flip the outcome of any round.

iPadOS macOS Hand Tracking Rhythm Game Camera AVFoundationVision Framework
Try on App Store ↗
Process GraBeat has two modes at its core — players competing to outscore each other, or collaborating to keep a shared Groove Meter alive together. The design challenge was making both feel distinct without splitting into two completely different UIs. Special notes that can flip a round's outcome needed to be instantly readable mid-air, with hands moving. The decision I'm most proud of: using motion and colour together to signal note type — so players learn the language in their very first round, with no tutorial needed.

02
Spottit!
Spottit!
Project Manager · UI/UX Designer
Check the room before you walk in.

Before you commit to going somewhere, Spottit! tells you what's actually happening there. Live space activity — Quiet, Crowded, or Collab in progress — synced instantly via CloudKit across every device. No refresh. No guessing.

iOSCloudKitReal-timeShared Spaces
Process Making "real-time" feel instant without a manual refresh was the core UX problem. Users needed to check a space and decide within seconds. The decision I'm most proud of: collapsing all possible space states into exactly three words — Quiet, Crowded, Collab — that anyone reads at a glance with no legend needed.

Shift
Shift
UI Designer · App Icon Designer
Your body called. Answer it.

Pick your window — 30 seconds to five minutes. Seated or on your feet. Shift selects the exercises, times your reps, and keeps you moving without breaking your focus. You'll feel the difference by the third one.

iOSWellnessMindfulnessMovement
Process The hardest UX problem wasn't designing the exercises — it was making the interruption feel welcome rather than annoying. A break app only works if people don't dismiss it. The decision I'm most proud of: the seated vs. standing split on the very first screen — one choice that personalises the entire session with zero onboarding friction.

Pass the Plot
UX Designer · Researcher · Co-Team Manager
Nobody remembers how it started.

A local multiplayer game where stories spiral gloriously out of control. Write a prompt. Pass it. Someone draws it. Someone else writes what they see. Repeat until the chain is unrecognisable and everyone's crying laughing. No accounts, no internet, no servers — just your devices and the inevitable chaos.

iOSMultiplayerOfflineAccessibleApp Store
App Store ↗
Process Designing for deliberate chaos without breaking the experience was the central tension — the game needed to spiral, but never crash. The decision I'm most proud of: going fully offline and peer-to-peer. No accounts, no servers, no friction. Players trusted it instantly, and that trust made them bolder with their prompts.

Designed around you, not your condition.

Smart reminders and interactive exercises that help you stay awake and in control. Track your patterns, customise your alerts, and build a day that works around your condition — not despite it.

iOSHealthNotificationsDark UI
